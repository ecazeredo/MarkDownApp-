This privacy policy applies to the MarkDown++ Editor app for Windows, published by ECAzeredo.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

MarkDown++ Editor does not collect, store, send or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services.

Your files

The app only reads and writes the files and folders you choose to open or save. Your files stay on your computer and are never uploaded or sent anywhere.

Pictures inside documents

If a document you open includes a picture hosted on a website, the app downloads that picture from that website to display it, the same way a web browser would. No information about you or your documents is sent to us.

Settings stored on your computer

The app remembers a few display preferences, such as zoom level and the width of the file list. These are stored only on your computer.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, visit the Support page.
