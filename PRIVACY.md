# MarkDown++ Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

MarkDown++ is a desktop app for viewing and editing Markdown files on your computer.

**MarkDown++ does not collect, store, send or share any personal data.**

- The app works entirely on your computer and never sends your data anywhere.
- If a document you open shows pictures hosted on a website, those pictures are downloaded from that website to display them, as a web browser would.
- Files you open or save stay on your computer. The app only reads and writes the files and folders you choose.
- The app remembers a few display preferences (zoom level and file list width) on your computer only.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party services.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ecazeredo/MarkDownApp-/issues
